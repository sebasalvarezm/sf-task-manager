# Process Manufacturing

## Group Overview

**End Markets:** Food and beverage manufacturers, dairy processors, pulp, paper and packaging converters, chemicals, coatings and plastics producers, nutraceutical and personal care manufacturers, co-packers.

**Workflows:** Ingredient and supplier quality (certificates, specifications, supplier audits), recipe and formula management, batch and production execution, OEE and downtime, quality and SPC on the line, lab data (LIMS), weight and fill control, lot traceability and recall, food safety (HACCP, FSMA 204, SQF and BRC audit readiness), labelling and allergen compliance.

**Types of Solutions:** Process ERP with recipe and lot control, batch and process MES, SPC and quality intelligence, LIMS and lab-to-line quality, food safety and compliance software, specification and formulation management (food PLM), supplier quality software, checkweigher and fill-control software.

**Routing notes (for classification):** This group is for software that runs the process plant, whatever goes into it. Software tied to the animal, crop or produce itself (herd management, grain elevators, packing and grading) belongs in Agriculture. Software that moves product between sites (TMS, warehousing, supply chain planning and forecasting, retail distribution) belongs in Logistics — Freight Supply Chain Enablement. Discrete manufacturing (machined parts, first article inspection, job shops) belongs in Manufacturing — Production Quality.

## Core Outreach Paragraph

We are building a dedicated Process Manufacturing group. The vision is to become a long-term home for the software that runs process plants in food and beverage, dairy, paper and packaging, and chemicals, from the ingredients and specifications coming in, through recipe and batch execution, quality, SPC and lab data on the line, to lot traceability, food safety and audit compliance going out. In simple terms, we help manufacturers make a consistent product every batch and prove it to auditors and customers, with less waste and fewer manual steps. Key steps in this effort were our acquisitions of QiSoft, a quality and production intelligence platform used by food, dairy and paper manufacturers across Europe and North America, and Matrix Industrial Control Systems, an MES for meat and dairy processors, and we are actively adding more software across process manufacturing.
