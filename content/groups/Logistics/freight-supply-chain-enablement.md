# Freight & Supply Chain Enablement

## Group Overview

**End Markets:** Software vendors serving logistics and supply chain operations across any mode or industry, including transportation and freight operators, warehouse and distribution operators, and procurement and sourcing teams. This is the horizontal layer of the Logistics group, not tied to a single mode.

**Workflows:** Transportation management, warehouse and inventory management, supplier and vendor management and sourcing, freight visibility and track-and-trace, yard and workforce coordination.

**Types of Solutions:** Multi-modal TMS, warehouse management systems (WMS) and inventory management, supplier and vendor management and sourcing software, freight visibility and track-and-trace platforms.

---

## Core Outreach Paragraph

We are building a dedicated Freight and Supply Chain Enablement group, the horizontal layer of our broader logistics vision. Rather than being tied to a single mode or industry, this group is a long-term home for the software categories that apply across the entire value chain, including transportation management, warehouse and inventory management, supplier and vendor management and sourcing, freight visibility and track-and-trace, and yard and workforce coordination. In simple terms, we help operators plan, move, store, and source across any mode and any industry, with fewer manual steps and one less IT vendor to manage.
